Apply to write for SymbiQ

SymbiQ runs on one strict rule: the desk decides what publishes, but the desk doesn’t have to write it alone. If you work in quantum computing, operations research or post-quantum security — as a researcher, a practitioner, or a serious enthusiast — you can apply to publish an Insight Note here under your own name.

What this is

A place for short, evidence-tiered essays — not a feed, not a forum. Every note gets the same 🟢🟡🔴 tier discipline and citation standard the rest of this site holds itself to, reviewed before it runs, credited to you, never edited into something you didn’t say. See Insight Notes for what one looks like, and why it might be worth your time specifically if you’re a working scientist or researcher.

How it works

1. You apply

Tell the desk who you are and what you’d want to write about. No account, no upload — the form below, or email.

2. It gets read

SymbiQ is one person. A reply won’t be instant, but the desk reads every application itself.

3. You write, the desk publishes it under your name

An approved pitch becomes a draft; the desk checks it against the same citation and tier rules every page here follows, then it goes up credited to you.

What to expect

  • No payment. This isn’t a content mill and it isn’t pretending to be one. What you get is a citable, dated, evidence-tiered byline under your own name — however small an audience this site has today.
  • Edited for the same things every page here is edited for: does the citation say what it’s cited for, does the tier match the evidence, is the claim falsifiable. Not for voice — that stays yours.
  • A “not yet” is possible and isn’t personal. It usually means the pitch needs to be sharper or more specific, and the desk will say which.
