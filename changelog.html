What changed

Four streams, one timeline, generated from the record rather than written by hand: every claim the Ledger takes on or rules on, every correction published, every story on The Signal, and every commit to the site itself.

The commits are the least interesting entries here and the most convincing ones. A corrections log tells you a desk admits its mistakes; a commit stream tells you the desk is still at the bench. Both matter, and neither is worth much without the other.
