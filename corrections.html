Found something wrong? Tell us.

This is the single most valuable thing you can send us. There is no points economy behind that sentence and we are not going to invent one: what you get is your name on the correction, published with more room than the claim it fixes. That is deliberate — a reader who checks our work is worth far more to us than a reader who trusts it.

You don't need to be certain, and you don't need to be polite about it. "This number looks off" is a perfectly good report. If you turn out to be right, the correction runs with your name on it.

Read by a human, always. If you'd rather do it in the open, post it in Discord — public catches are just as welcome, and easier for everyone else to learn from.

How we try to avoid needing this page

Every 🔴 topic gets an independent hostile-referee verification pass before it ships. Facts from secondary coverage get spot-checked against primary sources (arXiv, company blogs). It is not a formality: those passes have returned hundreds of findings, and every one was fixed before the page went out — which is why the log above starts at zero rather than where our own editing left off. Our own catches were never the point of this page; a reader's catch is.
