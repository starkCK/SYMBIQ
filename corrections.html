Nothing here yet, and that’s expected. The first entry on this page will be the first mistake a reader finds after we launch. It will run louder than whatever we got wrong, and it will carry your name if you want it there — see below.

Every entry follows the same three lines — what we said → what’s true → why we missed it. Corrections found by readers are credited by name with the 🏅 “taught it better than us” tag, and run with more room than the claim they fix.

Found something wrong? Tell us.

This is the single most valuable thing you can send us, and it carries the largest reward on the server — +150 Fidelity, more than any question, streak or raid pays. That is deliberate: a reader who checks our work is worth far more to us than a reader who trusts it.

You don't need to be certain, and you don't need to be polite about it. "This number looks off" is a perfectly good report. If you turn out to be right, the correction runs with your name on it.

Read by a human, always. If you'd rather do it in the open, post it in Discord — public catches are just as welcome, and easier for everyone else to learn from.

How we try to avoid needing this page

Every 🔴 post and Boss Question solution gets an independent hostile-referee verification pass before publishing. Facts from secondary coverage get spot-checked against primary sources (arXiv, company blogs). It is not a formality: those passes have already returned hundreds of findings, all fixed pre-launch — which is why the log above starts at zero rather than where our own editing left off. Publication-time catches were never the point of this page; a reader's catch is.
