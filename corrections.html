Found something wrong? Tell us.

This is the single most valuable thing you can send us, and it carries the largest reward on the server — +150 Fidelity, more than any question, streak or raid pays. That is deliberate: a reader who checks our work is worth far more to us than a reader who trusts it.

You don't need to be certain, and you don't need to be polite about it. "This number looks off" is a perfectly good report. If you turn out to be right, the correction runs with your name on it.

Read by a human, always. If you'd rather do it in the open, post it in Discord — public catches are just as welcome, and easier for everyone else to learn from.

How we try to avoid needing this page

Every 🔴 post and Boss Question solution gets an independent hostile-referee verification pass before publishing. Facts from secondary coverage get spot-checked against primary sources (arXiv, company blogs). The verification log lives in our internal records with dates and sources.
