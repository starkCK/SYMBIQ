The Frontier

The questions the field has not settled — timelines nobody can falsify yet, bottlenecks that might or might not bind, the routes from quantum to better AI that are still arguments. Each one is sent verbatim to a panel of frontier AI models and published next to their answers and the desk's own reviewed one. Nothing here is answered yet — this page is the scaffold: the questions are real, the model panel has not been run, and the reviewed answers are not written. That is the honest starting state.

How The Frontier works — and the constitution it has to respect

SymbiQ's locked editorial rule is that nothing user-generated auto-publishes. Peer review, replies and live threads cannot live on the same surface as that rule, so The Frontier uses two:

  • The reviewed answer — canonical, evidence-tiered, sourced, permanent, crawlable. Written by the desk, checked by a second person before it ships (the same two-key rule the Ledger uses). It lives on this page.
  • The Floor — the live argument: replies, reactions, people changing their minds. That happens in Discord, one auto-created thread per question, run by the existing mods. This page shows a summary of it and a link in; it never hosts the comments itself.

The Floor is not wired yet — the Discord link goes live when the server is connected. Until then a question has its background, its model panel, and (once written) its reviewed answer.

The model panel

Each question goes to several frontier models under one public, identical prompt. Every answer is published verbatim, tagged with the exact model version and the date it was asked — a leaderboard against a family name is meaningless six weeks later. An answer is an entrant, graded by the desk on correctness, calibration, sourcing and hedging — never presented as authoritative. Scoring AI models on quantum reasoning is not a gimmick here: the loop between AI and quantum is the whole subject of this site.

Know a question that belongs here?

It has to be genuinely open — not something the site already answers — stated in one sentence, and falsifiable in principle even if not yet in practice. Nothing publishes automatically; the desk opens it as a real question, and sends it to the panel, only if it clears that bar.
