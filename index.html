Error / round — pL Physical qubits — 2d² − 1 Lifetime — before one error

Under the hood: pL = 0.1 · (p/pth)⌊(d+1)/2⌋ with pth = 1% (Fowler et al. 2012), a rotated surface-code patch at 2d²−1 physical qubits, one syndrome round per microsecond. Wrong if your hardware's threshold is not 1% or its round is not 1 µs — either moves the lifetime by orders of magnitude. The full explorer ▸

The fork: same circuit, the other branch

Operations research: the field that already decides your day

Every quantum-optimisation headline you have read is secretly a claim about operations research, the mathematics of choosing well when you can't have everything. The field is a century old and already runs under your day like wiring behind plaster. Without it, you have no way to judge a single quantum-optimisation claim.

It routed your parcelAnd staffed tonight's hospital shift, and priced your flight twice while you looked at it.
It runs inside the quantum computerSurface-code decoding is minimum-weight matching (Edmonds, 1965), so an OR algorithm sits inside the machine as a working part.
It is what the hype is aboutEvery “quantum will transform optimisation” pitch is a claim about this field. Learn the field and the pitch becomes testable.
It is more learnable than it looksSchool algebra and one drawing get you the core idea. The first step is shorter than the field's reputation suggests.

So it gets its own front door. Come for the theory on its own terms, then keep it as the ruler every quantum claim on this site gets measured against.
