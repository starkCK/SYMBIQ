How it works here

A meme a day (the door). A question a day: answers sit in superposition — spoiler-tagged — until the nightly Collapse, when the Answer of the Day is named and credited. On weekends the whole community raids the Boss Question together: it has hit points, and verified partial results deal damage. Everything you earn grows The Lattice — the community's shared code distance, displayed right here as it climbs.

SymbiQ is edited, not crowdsourced: member submissions are reviewed by the desk, and the best ship to the main feeds with your name on them. Catching an error in our content pays the biggest bounty on the server. A weekly scoreboard tracks the six labs racing to fault tolerance (The Race) — including our own prediction record, kept publicly. When we get something wrong, the correction gets more prominence than the mistake (Corrections).
