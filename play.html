▶ The Solver's Path

The Arcade

The same five machines you meet on the Solver's Path — but here there is no story, no mentor and no order. Just the games, and the physics doing the judging.

Push the door

Behind it: five machines that will not let you fake a score. Where a machine has a par — Circuit Golf, Grover's Escape, Max-Cut — that par is a proven optimum found by exhaustive search, not a designer's guess. The Volcano scores you on a schedule replayed 500 times rather than on a par, because a single lucky run proves nothing. Every probability on every screen is computed rather than chosen. And one landscape is built to prove that your best strategy is worthless on the wrong problem.

New — rebuilt

The whole campaign, rebuilt as scenes

The same verified engines as the machines below, inside the thing we are building instead of a widget in a box: a world that responds to the number the game is actually computing, and mentors whose flaws are the mechanic rather than a paragraph about them.

Six acts, five to eight minutes each. Sound is off until you ask for it. On The Solver’s Path they run in order and remember what you did; here you can walk into any one of them.

Out in the wild

Five more playable things live on the pages they belong to, because each one only makes sense standing next to its explanation.

The Decoder Duel

Ten rounds against the textbook matching decoder, on a chip carrying a defect nobody told it about. You sit in the AI's seat.

The CHSH game

The Question

One question at a time, answered at three levels, with a streak that forgives a single miss the way a real code corrects a single error.

The Bottleneck

Five upgrades, three resources, and a shadow price that lies to you if you read it without its range. Operations research, on The Feasible Region.

The Prune

Prove which haul is best by opening about thirty-five branches out of thousands — and finish holding a certificate rather than a guess.

Coming next

A full board game for two to four players, on quantum computing and the parts of AI that actually touch it — where knowing more genuinely wins, and the board, pieces and animation are built to be worth looking at. Designed, not yet started, and deliberately behind the rebuild of the machines above; we would rather say that than call it "in development" for another six months. The five machines are the groundwork: each is a mechanic that will earn its place on that board, or be cut.
