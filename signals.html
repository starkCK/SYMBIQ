How The Signal works

When it runs, a daily pass over a short list of primary sources will draft at most one story, never a roundup or filler for a quiet day. The draft cannot state any number that is not in the source, has to name what a reader would wrongly conclude from the headline, and is checked against everything SymbiQ already teaches, so a claim about a new decoder result links straight to the page that explains decoders. Nothing publishes on its own: it becomes an approval request, and only a reply of post ships it.

How this feed opened, told with the same candour. The automated pass has never run — it needs credentials the repository does not have — and for weeks this page shipped with nothing on it but the words “still warming up”. That was worse than an empty space, on a site that argues about the difference between a plan and a result. So the five stories above were written by hand on 8 September 2026, in the same format the pipeline produces, from primary sources published between 31 July and 8 September. They are a backfill, not a day's output, and they are dated the day they were written rather than the day their sources broke. When the daily pass runs, it adds at most one story a day on top of these.

Nothing here auto-publishes.
