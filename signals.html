How these notes are written

Each note is written by hand, dated the day it was written, and covers one story. Sources are primary: the paper, the announcement or the filing. AI drafts the first version; scripts check the numbers it quotes, and Chinmoy approves every note before it is published. How we check says what each of those catches and misses.

There is no schedule and no automation behind this page. A note appears when a story is worth one, and some weeks there is none. Each note names its evidence tier and says what a reader might wrongly conclude from the headline.

The first five notes were all written on 8 September 2026 from sources published between 31 July and 8 September, so they are dated the day they were written, not the day their sources broke.
