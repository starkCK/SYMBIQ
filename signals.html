How The Signal is meant to work — and why it is not

The design. A daily pass over a short list of primary sources drafts at most one story — never a roundup, never filler for a quiet day. The draft is forbidden from stating any number that is not in the source, must name what a reader would wrongly conclude from the headline, and is checked against everything SymbiQ already teaches so a claim about, say, a new decoder result links straight to the page that explains decoders. None of that publishes on its own: it becomes an approval request, and only a reply of post ships it.

Status, 22 August 2026: it is not running. The scheduled job exists and fires every morning, and it has failed on every run since the day it was created — it stops at its first step, before it ever reads a source, because a credential it needs was never configured. So there is no queue of drafts waiting on a human here; there are no drafts at all. This paragraph comes down when the first Signal goes up.
