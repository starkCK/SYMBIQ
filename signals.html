The Signal

One AI/quantum story at a time, written in the loop's voice. Every post names its evidence tier, links straight to whatever it rests on that SymbiQ already teaches, and carries a "what this does NOT mean" section as standard — not a footnote. A human reads and approves every story before it ships; nothing here is auto-published.

How The Signal works

A daily pass over a short list of primary sources drafts at most one story — never a roundup, never filler for a quiet day. The draft is forbidden from stating any number that is not in the source, must name what a reader would wrongly conclude from the headline, and is checked against everything SymbiQ already teaches so a claim about, say, a new decoder result links straight to the page that explains decoders. None of that publishes on its own: it becomes an approval request, and only a reply of post ships it.

Nothing here auto-publishes. Every Signal is approved by a human before it ships.
